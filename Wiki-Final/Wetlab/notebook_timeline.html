    7 月下旬起，团队从往年优秀项目和文献出发建立候选问题池：群体感应、微生物燃料、生物传感器、PFAS、原位资源利用、宇航员生物钟、太空食物等都曾进入讨论。8 月门老师介绍太空赛道与微流控，团队意识到自身能接触太空相关导师、地基模拟装置与微流控资源，太空赛道逐渐成为优先方向。

  • Sep – Nov 2025

    Three parallel directions三个方向并行探索

    Offline meetings crystallized three parallel groups: algae-bacteria co-culture (oxygen & food symbiosis), space waste utilization (organics → medium / building material / fuel, later including keratin hydrogels from waste hair), and omega-3 nutritional supplements. Valuable modules, but no single through-line yet — the real topic-selection process is complex and iterative.

    9 月线下组会后探索分化为三个并行小组：藻菌共培养（产氧与食物共生体系）、太空废弃物利用（有机物转培养基/建材/燃料，10 月起包含废弃毛发角蛋白水凝胶方向）与 omega-3 营养补充。有价值的模块很多，但尚未形成主线——真实的选题过程本就复杂而反复。

  • Dec 2025 – Jan 2026

    The Aspergillus niger platform & composting fieldwork黑曲霉平台与堆肥实践

    Directions were reorganized into a "space solid-waste bio-recycling platform" with Aspergillus niger as the chassis. We stress-tested it honestly — degradation efficiency, oxygen demand, safety, metabolic burden. In January, pre-experiments and composting practice in Longhua exposed the real problems: turning, stirring, microbial-community complexity and inconsistent standards. Mass transfer stopped being an abstract concept.

    12 月，多类方向重组为"太空固体废弃物生物回收—培养平台"，黑曲霉因分泌能力强成为核心候选底盘，团队同时明确列出其必要性、降解效率、耗氧、安全性等风险清单。2026 年 1 月预实验与龙华堆肥实践暴露具体问题：翻堆、搅拌、菌群复杂性和标准不统一。"传质"从抽象背景变成可感知的工程问题。

  • 2026Convergence on microgravity mass transfer · 收敛于微重力传质问题
  • Mar 2, 2026

    The turning point: chemotaxis in low convection关键转向：低对流环境中的趋化

    The end-of-holiday meeting became the project's decisive turn. Comparing fungi and bacteria, we kept the chemotaxis concept with a bacterial chassis: mature tools, short cycles. The new logic — cellulose is partially degraded, locally releasing cellobiose; engineered bacteria sense this local signal and accumulate near the substrate in diffusion-dominated, low-convection regions. Bacterial biased random walks don't need an absolute gravity direction.

    3 月 2 日假期收尾会议成为项目的关键转向：比较真菌与细菌后，决定保留趋化设定、采用细菌底盘（工具成熟、周期短）。新逻辑：纤维素被初步降解后局部释放纤维二糖等可溶性产物，工程菌利用局部信号调整运动，在对流弱、扩散占主导的区域向底物靠近——细菌的偏置随机游走不需要绝对重力方向。

  • Mar 6 – 19, 2026

    Four-layer hypothesis & the first chemotaxis simulator四层假设与第一版趋化模拟器

    We decomposed the idea into four testable layers: is chemotaxis more important under microgravity; does enhanced chemotaxis improve substrate utilization; can chemotaxis be engineered; can engineered strains show a measurable difference. On Mar 19 we decided to start with MG1655, behavioral/chemical surrogate validation and a Python model, so material access wouldn't block progress. The modeling work also produced the first interactive chemotaxis simulator mini-game.

    3 月 6—15 日，设想被拆成四层假设：微重力下趋化是否更重要、增强趋化是否改善底物利用、趋化能否被工程化、工程菌能否表现出可比较差异。3 月 19 日决定先用 MG1655、行为/化学替代验证和 Python 模型推进，避免被材料获取限制进度。建模工作同时催生了第一版交互式可视化趋化模拟器小游戏，为教育传播提供了工具。

  • Mar 30 – 31, 2026

    Retrospective: one problem, one through-line阶段复盘：围绕一个问题组织全队

    The stage retrospective explicitly mapped our iteration chain — algae-bacteria → keratin → A. niger degradation → composting → microgravity chemotaxis — and confirmed that past parallelism had been excessive. Modeling, HP, education, visuals, wiki and wet lab would now all organize around one question: cellobiose PTS sensing with positive feedback.

    3 月 30—31 日阶段回顾第一次把路线转折明确总结为"藻菌—角蛋白—黑曲霉降解—堆肥—微重力趋化"的迭代链路，确认过去并行策略过多，需要围绕一个问题组织建模、HP、教育、视觉、Wiki 和湿实验。纤维二糖 PTS 与正反馈由此成为主线。

  • Apr 2026

    MCP → PTS, and a mechanism error caught in reviewMCP 转向 PTS，机制错误被文档审查发现

    MCP receptors could describe motion but not how cellobiose enters the sensing-metabolism chain, so we pivoted to PTS. An early draft's bglF error (it prefers aromatic β-glucosides, not cellobiose) was caught by our own document-review mechanism. After comparing bgl / chb / asc, the plan formally switched to ascF + ascB on Apr 5–7 — with a documented risk: even activated asc may not support growth on cellobiose as sole carbon source.

    MCP 路线能描述运动行为，却没解决"纤维二糖怎样进入感知和代谢链"，4 月初团队转向 PTS。初期草案的 bglF 机制错误（不能识别纤维二糖，更偏向芳香族 β-葡萄糖苷）被团队文件整理机制及时审查发现。重新比较 bgl、chb 与 asc 后，4 月 5—7 日方案正式转为 ascF+ascB，同时记录在案的风险是：即使 asc 被激活，菌株也可能无法在以纤维二糖为唯一碳源的培养基中生长。

On May 24 at the South China exchange, our booth, talk and mini-game debuted together; other teams spoke highly of the game format, and the exchanges pushed our own thinking forward.

    Cloning, sequencing, first engineered strains载体组装、测序与首批工程菌

After finals, work resumed Jul 9–11: the ascFB engineered strain was obtained and banked.

    5 月 28 日后进入载体组装、转化、单克隆筛选、PCR 与测序。6 月 8 日候选克隆 PCR 条带较好；6 月 10—11 日测序返回，团队检查移码与额外突变后，将无明显问题的构建转入 MG1655。期末后 7 月 9—11 日恢复实验，获得 ascFB 工程菌并保种。

  • Jul 14, 2026

    Expert interview reframes the claim专家访谈修正项目主张

    Visiting SIAT and interviewing microgravity-simulation experts changed our core claim: chemotaxis should not be presented as a replacement for macroscopic stirring. Stirring handles reactor-scale mass transfer; chemotaxis, if it works, contributes in boundary layers, pores and low-flow regions where stirring cannot fully remove local diffusion limits. Microfluidics was repositioned as a platform for constructing and measuring controlled low-convection environments.

    7 月 14 日团队参访先进院并采访微重力模拟专家，项目主张被直接改变：趋化不应被描述为宏观搅拌的替代品——搅拌负责反应器尺度整体传质；趋化若能成立，贡献应体现在边界层、孔隙和低流速区域等搅拌难以完全消除局部扩散限制的地方。微流控被重新定位为控制/减弱对流、测量局部富集的实验平台。

  • Jul 15 – 20, 2026

    The negative result that started a new cycle开启新循环的阴性结果

    Cellobiose metabolism validation began Jul 15. On Jul 19 the engineered strain failed to grow — and the team immediately designated this as the new DBTL starting point rather than hiding it. Re-streaking and controls were set up the same day; on Jul 20, SDS-PAGE showed a distinct ~55 kDa band for ascFB versus empty vector — the construction/expression chain was partially intact. The question moved from "is it expressed at all" to "is it expressed correctly with substrate flux".

    7 月 15 日起团队先做纤维二糖代谢验证。7 月 19 日报告工程菌未养起，团队随即将该结果定为新的 DBTL 起点；当天重新划线纯化工程菌与空载体。7 月 20 日 SDS-PAGE 显示 ascFB 工程菌相较空载体在约 55 kDa 处出现明显条带——构建/表达链条部分成立。排查重心从"是否完全没表达"推进到"表达是否正确并形成底物通量"。

  • Jul 22 – 30, 2026

    Systematic troubleshooting → literature verdict: pivot to chb系统排查 → 文献复盘：转向 chb

    TTC metabolic staining, hard-agar plug vs. capillary assay design, buffer pH checks, 12-h growth curves — every candidate explanation was tested. On Jul 30 the literature review delivered the mechanism: AscF alone cannot support efficient cellobiose transport; asc activity is only ~1/3 of chb, and published efficient strains combine both systems with serial passaging. This met the risk documented back in April — not operator error, but a mechanistic limit. The main line switched to the chb operon.

    TTC 代谢显色、硬琼脂塞法与毛细管法方案比较、缓冲体系 pH 排查、12 小时生长曲线——候选解释被逐一验证。7 月 30 日文献复盘给出机制解释：AscF 单独不足以完成纤维二糖高效转运，asc 酶活仅约为 chb 的 1/3，已报道的高效菌株需两套系统搭配并传代培养。这与 4 月选择 asc 时记录在案的风险相遇——不是操作失误，而是机制限制。主线正式转向 chb 操纵子。

  • Aug 22 – 31, 2026

    chb plasmid v1: pRSFDuet-1 + CP promoter ladderchb 质粒第一版：pRSFDuet-1 + CP 梯度启动子

    Gene scope fixed from the genomic order chbBCARFH: use chbBCAF, skip regulator R and H. Version 1 rode on pRSFDuet-1 with a five-step constitutive CP promoter ladder (incl.     从基因组原始顺序 chbBCARFH 中确定取舍：取用 chbBCAF，不使用 R 与 H。第一版以 pRSFDuet-1 为骨架，用 CP 系列五档梯度组成型启动子（含 J23113、J23116）驱动表达——梯度设计意在滴定表达强度、规避膜转运蛋白毒性。8 月 31 日转化板开始长出菌落，挑单克隆扩培并送测序。

  • Sep 8 – 18, 2026

    Plasmid route fails twice → genome engineering质粒路线两度受挫 → 转向基因组改造

    Parallel λ-Red preparation: pKD13 arrived Sep 8, Gibson assembly enzyme Sep 16, electroporation cuvettes Sep 17, sequencing primers designed and ordered. But on Sep 18, even the low-copy pET-28 version yielded only empty-vector colonies. High-copy failed, low-copy failed — membrane-protein burden from plasmid-borne chb was the suspected mechanism. The plasmid route was formally closed; the team went all-in on chromosomal promoter replacement via pKD46/λ-Red.

    基因组改造线并行准备：9 月 8 日 pKD13 到货、16 日 Gibson 组装酶到货、17 日电击杯到货，测序引物完成设计订购。但 9 月 18 日，换用低拷贝 pET-28 骨架后依旧只有空载体长菌——高拷贝失败、降拷贝仍失败，疑似质粒承载的 chb 膜蛋白对宿主负担/毒性过大。质粒路线正式关闭，全面转向 pKD46/λ-Red 染色体启动子替换。

  • Sep 17 – 18, 2026

    Microfluidic chemotaxis chip: design review with SIAT微流控趋化芯片：与先进院设计对接

    Armed with a BioProtocol-based three-channel perfusion design, we met Mr. Tom Xu to adapt it to SIAT's platform: two-channel perfusion with peristaltic (not syringe) pumps, RPM-compatible. His suggestion — exploit the paper's intermittent perfusion: no flow while rotating, manual perfusion when stopped. Device constraints became design inputs.

    团队带着文献方案（BioProtocol 细菌趋化定量，三通道灌流设计）与先进院 Tom Xu 老师讨论装置适配：现有平台为两路灌流、蠕动泵（非注射泵），RPM 上暂只能适配蠕动泵。徐老师建议利用文中间歇灌流特点——旋转时不灌流，停止后手动灌流再启动。装置约束由此转化为设计输入。

  • Sep 20 – 23, 2026

    CP12 grows on cellobiose — first positive function 🎉CP12 纤维二糖生长曲线阳性——首个功能证据

    On Sep 20, electroporation-derived cp12 colonies appeared; clones were PCR-verified and sequenced (strain series cp6/7/12/25/30/44 against WT MG1655). On Sep 23 the growth curve came back: with cellobiose as sole carbon source, CP12 showed clear growth over wild type — slower than glucose, but unmistakably positive. The first functional evidence since the project began: our engineered strain really does use cellobiose.

    9 月 20 日电转后 cp12 候选菌落长出，菌落 PCR 验证后送测序（菌株系列 cp6/7/12/25/30/44，以野生型 MG1655 为基线）。9 月 23 日生长曲线结果返回：纤维二糖为唯一碳源时，CP12 相较野生型有明显增长——比葡萄糖慢很多，但确凿阳性。这是立项以来第一个功能性阳性证据：工程菌真的开始利用纤维二糖了。

  • Oct 6, 2026

    Soft-agar chemotaxis: honest mixed results软琼脂趋化实验：如实记录的结果

    CP-series soft-agar plates (1% tryptone, 0.35% agar; 10 mM cellobiose / glucose / sugar-free) read out: WT and CP12 formed the largest migration zones; other CP strains stayed small — no detectable engineered chemotaxis gain. Learn: swarm-zone size conflates chemotaxis, growth and motility, and a uniform attractant creates no spatial gradient. The result went into the DBTL in full, and defined the next design: known-attractant controls, growth-curve decoupling, and true gradients on microfluidics.

    CP 系列软琼脂趋化平板（1% 胰蛋白胨 + 0.35% 琼脂；10 mM 纤维二糖/葡萄糖/无糖三条件）读板：野生型与 CP12 迁移圈近似最大，其他 CP 工程菌圈较小——未观察到工程化趋化增益。机制分析：迁移圈是趋化、生长与运动能力的叠加，均匀添加的吸引剂不构成空间梯度。该结果完整写入 DBTL，并引出下一步设计：已知吸引物对照、结合生长曲线区分贡献、用微流控构建真实空间梯度。

  • Oct 17 – 18, 2026

    Microfluidic chip completed微流控芯片制作完成

    Oct 16 revealed the blocker: bacteria invisible against an agar background under the microscope.
